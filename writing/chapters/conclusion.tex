\textbf{Summary.} \gracefull{} (\gracename{}) addresses heterogeneous VLM
distillation by combining Byte-Trie Wasserstein output matching, sparse
top-\(k\) teacher routing and gradient-agreement refinement
\cite{boizard2024towardscrosstokenizerdistillation,peyre2019computationaloptimaltransport,fedus2022switchtransformersscalingtrillion}.
Together these three components resolve tokenizer mismatch, adapt teacher
selection per sample and filter KD gradients that conflict with the
supervised CE objective. On TextVQA, DocVQA and ChartQA, \methodname{}
outperforms same-family, single-teacher and heterogeneous multi-teacher
baselines, reaching 69.38, 73.11 and 80.20 with SmolVLM-500M and 60.73,
60.48 and 72.32 with SmolVLM-256M. \methodname{} closes about 55\% of the
teacher-student gap on TextVQA and ChartQA and about 40\% on DocVQA,
suggesting that supervision design is a greater bottleneck than model
capacity at this scale.

\textbf{Limitations.} Three limitations constrain the scope of the current results. \textbf{Cost-limited evaluation.} The single-run design precludes uncertainty estimates for small gaps between
high-scoring baselines. An average run costs approximately 14.41 USD
(Section~\ref{sec:efficiency-analysis}), excluding caching cost. 

\textbf{Scope of validation.} The study covers VQA benchmarks and
SmolVLM-family students, while broader task families, other student architectures
and larger teacher pools are untested. Tasks with long-form answers,
multi-image inputs, video reasoning or open-ended dialogue need different
forms of teacher selection and answer supervision.

\textbf{Reasoning-oriented supervision.}
GRACE uses teacher output distributions at answer tokens as its distillation
signal, filtered by gradient agreement with the supervised loss. This fits the VQA setting, where targets are short final answers, but does not
transfer intermediate reasoning traces or accommodate models that generate
extended reasoning at test time. Handling this requires routing teachers by intermediate reasoning quality
and distilling from multi-step traces, not just final-answer distributions.

\textbf{Future Work.} The most immediate need is statistical: repeated runs
with multiple seeds would establish which score differences between
multi-teacher baselines are reliable. On the systems side, more compact
logit storage and lower-cost agreement scoring would make scaling to larger
teacher pools practical. Extending \methodname{} beyond output-level
supervision to visual grounding via token alignment or cross-attention
transfer and to reasoning-oriented models where intermediate traces must be
distilled alongside final answers are the two substantive open directions
\cite{yuan2020reinforcedmultiteacherselection,zhang2023adaptivemultiteachermetalearning,yu2024selectdistill}.
