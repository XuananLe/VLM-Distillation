\chapter*{Conclusion and Future Work}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Conclusion and Future Work}

This chapter summarizes the main contributions and the empirical conclusions of
the study. The main limitations are discussed separately in
Chapter~\ref{chap:experiment-results}. This final part focuses on the core
takeaways and on practical directions for future work.

We studied compact visual language model distillation under
heterogeneous teachers. The student is much smaller than the teacher set, the
teachers do not help every sample equally and their tokenizers do not match.
We present \methodname{}, a routed multi-teacher
framework with trie-Wasserstein cross-tokenizer distillation, sparse top-\(k\)
selection and EMA-smoothed GRACE weighting. The code follows that order:
route teachers first, refine their weights with GRACE scores, then aggregate
the routed trie-Wasserstein KD loss
\cite{boizard2024towardscrosstokenizerdistillation,peyre2019computationaloptimaltransport,fedus2022switchtransformersscalingtrillion}.

Empirically, \methodname{} is the strongest method in the main comparison. For
SmolVLM-500M, it reaches 69.38 on TextVQA, 73.11 on DocVQA and 80.20 on
ChartQA. For SmolVLM-256M, it reaches 60.73, 60.48 and 72.32. These results
improve over the original students, direct finetuning and the best competing
baselines in Chapter~\ref{chap:experiment-results}. On SmolVLM-500M,
\methodname{} closes about 55\% of the original teacher-student gap on TextVQA
and ChartQA and about 40\% on DocVQA. Taken together, these results establish that
selective routing plus post-routing score refinement is a strong recipe for
compact text-rich VLM distillation.

\section*{Future Work}
\begin{itemize}
\item Stronger evaluation. Repeated runs would make the comparison more rigorous, especially for the smaller gaps between the stronger multi-teacher baselines.
\item Better systems efficiency. The pipeline still relies on large offline teacher-logit caches, so reducing storage and I/O cost would make the method easier to scale to more datasets and larger teacher sets.
\item Broader method extensions. The recipe is still answer-token centric, so one important next step is explicit visual distillation: visual-token alignment, cross-attention transfer, or region-level matching. The method can also study richer router inputs, stronger objective-conflict handling and teacher sets that mix specialized document, chart and general VLM experts \cite{yuan2020reinforcedmultiteacherselection,zhang2023adaptivemultiteachermetalearning,yu2024selectdistill}.
\end{itemize}
