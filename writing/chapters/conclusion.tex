\chapter*{Conclusion and Future Work}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Conclusion and Future Work}

This thesis studied vision-language model distillation under heterogeneous
teachers, where teacher utility varies across samples and teacher-student
tokenizers do not match.
To address these challenges, we presented \gracefull{} (\gracename{}), a
routed multi-teacher framework with trie-Wasserstein tokenizer-agnostic
distillation, sparse top-\(k\) selection and gradient-agreement refinement.
The training procedure routes teachers first, refines their weights with
gradient-agreement scores and
then aggregates the routed trie-Wasserstein KD loss
\cite{boizard2024towardscrosstokenizerdistillation,peyre2019computationaloptimaltransport,fedus2022switchtransformersscalingtrillion}. Empirically, \methodname{} obtains the highest scores among the methods
compared in the main tables, confirming that selective routing and
post-routing gradient refinement improve heterogeneous teacher-to-student VLM
distillation under the evaluated VQA setting. For SmolVLM-500M, it
reaches 69.38 on TextVQA,
73.11 on DocVQA and 80.20 on ChartQA. For SmolVLM-256M, \methodname{} reaches
60.73 on TextVQA, 60.48 on DocVQA and 72.32 on ChartQA, showing that the
method improves both evaluated student models. These results
exceed the original students, direct
finetuning and all reported distillation baselines in
Chapter~\ref{chap:experiment-results}. On SmolVLM-500M,
\methodname{} closes about 55\% of the original teacher-student gap on TextVQA
and ChartQA and about 40\% on DocVQA.

\section*{Future Work}
Future work should investigate three directions that would strengthen the
empirical scope, reduce systems cost and extend the method beyond answer-token
distillation. First, \textbf{stronger evaluation}: repeated runs with multiple
seeds would tighten the comparison,
especially for the smaller gaps between close multi-teacher baselines.
Second, \textbf{systems efficiency}: the pipeline still relies on large offline
teacher-logit caches, so reducing storage and I/O cost would make the method
easier to scale to more datasets and larger teacher sets. Third,
\textbf{broader method extensions}: the current method remains
answer-token-centric, so one important next step is explicit visual
distillation through visual-token alignment, cross-attention transfer, or
region-level matching. Future work can also explore richer router inputs,
improved objective-conflict handling and teacher sets that mix specialized
document, chart and general VLM experts
\cite{yuan2020reinforcedmultiteacherselection,zhang2023adaptivemultiteachermetalearning,yu2024selectdistill}.
