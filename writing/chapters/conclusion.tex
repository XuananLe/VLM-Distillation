\chapter*{Conclusion and Future Work}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Conclusion and Future Work}

This thesis studied compact vision-language model distillation with
heterogeneous teachers. The student is much smaller than the teacher models,
teacher utility varies across samples and their tokenizers do not match.
To address these challenges, we presented \methodname{}, a routed multi-teacher
framework with trie-Wasserstein cross-tokenizer distillation, sparse top-\(k\)
selection and gradient-agreement refinement. The training procedure routes
teachers first, refines their weights with gradient-agreement scores and
then aggregates the routed trie-Wasserstein KD loss
\cite{boizard2024towardscrosstokenizerdistillation,peyre2019computationaloptimaltransport,fedus2022switchtransformersscalingtrillion}.

Empirically, \methodname{} obtains the highest scores among the methods
compared in the main tables. For SmolVLM-500M, it reaches 69.38 on TextVQA,
73.11 on DocVQA and 80.20 on ChartQA. For SmolVLM-256M, it reaches 60.73,
60.48 and 72.32. These results exceed the original students, direct
finetuning and all reported distillation baselines in
Chapter~\ref{chap:experiment-results}. On SmolVLM-500M,
\methodname{} closes about 55\% of the original teacher-student gap on TextVQA
and ChartQA and about 40\% on DocVQA. The results confirm that combining
selective routing with post-routing score refinement improves compact
text-rich VLM distillation under the benchmarks and teacher pool studied here.

\section*{Future Work}
Three directions merit further investigation. First, \textbf{stronger
evaluation}: repeated runs with multiple seeds would tighten the comparison,
especially for the smaller gaps between close multi-teacher baselines.
Second, \textbf{systems efficiency}: the pipeline still relies on large offline
teacher-logit caches, so reducing storage and I/O cost would make the method
easier to scale to more datasets and larger teacher sets. Third,
\textbf{broader method extensions}: the current method remains
answer-token-centric, so one important next step is explicit visual
distillation through visual-token alignment, cross-attention transfer, or
region-level matching. Future work can also explore richer router inputs,
improved objective-conflict handling and teacher sets that mix specialized
document, chart and general VLM experts
\cite{yuan2020reinforcedmultiteacherselection,zhang2023adaptivemultiteachermetalearning,yu2024selectdistill}.
