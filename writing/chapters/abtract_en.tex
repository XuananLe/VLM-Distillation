\textbf{\large{Abstract}}
\end{center}

\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
\begin{small}

We study heterogeneous multi-teacher distillation for vision-language models
(VLMs), where supervision is transferred from a pool of frozen teachers to a
student model. The setting is difficult because teachers come from different
model families, use different tokenizers and provide complementary supervision
across samples, which makes fixed teacher averaging unreliable.


We present \gracefull{} (\gracename{}), a framework designed to
address three failure modes in this setting: tokenizer mismatch, heterogeneous
teacher specialization and unstable post-routing weighting. The frozen
heterogeneous teachers are treated as tokenizer-specific experts. Teacher
assignment is gradient-routed: after sparse top-$k$ routing selects a
candidate set, routed weights are refined according to the agreement between
each teacher's KD gradient and the supervised CE gradient. To the best of our
knowledge, \gracename{} is the first distillation framework to use shared
byte-trie optimal transport for heterogeneous multi-teacher VLM distillation.
\gracename{} combines a trie-Wasserstein KD loss over a shared byte trie,
sparse top-$k$ teacher routing and gradient-agreement refinement. Routed
teacher weights are then derived from smoothed agreement scores over the
routed teacher set.


In TextVQA, DocVQA and ChartQA experiments with SmolVLM-500M and
SmolVLM-256M as student models, \methodname{} improves over direct finetuning
and all compared distillation baselines. In the four-teacher setting,
\methodname{} reaches 69.38, 73.11 and 80.20 on SmolVLM-500M and 60.73, 60.48
and 72.32 on SmolVLM-256M. For SmolVLM-500M, this corresponds to gains of
22.26\%, 22.83\% and 27.71\% over the original student. The method also closes
55.03\% of the original teacher gap on TextVQA, 39.53\% on DocVQA and 55.34\%
on ChartQA. These results demonstrate \methodname{} as an effective approach
to heterogeneous multi-teacher VLM distillation on text-rich VQA tasks.


\vspace*{1cm}
\textbf{Keywords}: vision-language model, knowledge distillation,
multi-teacher distillation, sparse teacher routing, gradient agreement,
cross-tokenizer Wasserstein distillation
\end{small}
